Sources, coverage and methods
Education — UNESCO Institute for Statistics data via the World Bank Indicators API: female and male gross tertiary/secondary enrolment, primary completion and adult literacy. Occupation — ILO-modelled gender-specific labour-market series via World Bank WDI. Indicator years can differ by source and country. World Bank API documentation · Gender Data Portal.
Income — UNDP historical gender-income estimates via Our World in Data (constant 2017 international-$), and UNDP Human Development Report 2025 gender-specific GNI estimates via Our World in Data (constant 2017 international-$; 2023 cross-section). These are estimates, not observed salaries. Annual mean employee earnings by sex now load automatically from the official ILOSTAT data service for the selected countries and period. Values remain in reported local currency; comparative charts use the female/male percentage gap. UNDP source table.

UNDP estimates from OWID and ILOSTAT employee earnings are distinct income measures with different units and provenance; they are never merged into one time series. The current site is an exploratory research atlas, not an official computed DYSIC index. No DYSIC composite scores or inferred causal coefficients are invented.
